About the event

WHAT IS A BEER MILE?

Four laps of 400 metres, with one drink after each lap. It's a test of speed, endurance and stomach strength — and above all, a lot of fun.

The Basel edition brings together runners, local breweries, non-alcoholic alternatives and the Basel community for an unforgettable event in one of Switzerland's most vibrant cities.
